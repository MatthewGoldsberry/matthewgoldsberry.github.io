## Overview

<figure class="mg-figure--aside">
  <img src="/assets/media/projects/habsat-1/mission-patch.png" alt="HABSat-1 mission patch" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
</figure>

HABSat-1 (**H**armful **A**lgae **B**loom **Sat**ellite 1) is UC CubeCats' second mission. Its goal is to capture imagery of harmful algae blooms, with a focus on the health of Lake Erie.

I lead the On-Board Computer (OBC) team, and our senior design project is the OBC's first prototype flight software: designing, implementing, and verifying it with CubeCats. That software provides the satellite's core Command and Data Handling (CDH), moving commands and telemetry between the camera, attitude control, and power subsystems and the ground station, and runs autonomous and scheduled tasks as the satellite moves through its flight states.

## Where it stands

**Current status:** We completed our Subsystems Requirement Review (SRR) in April 2026.

**What's next:** The OBC team is working toward a Preliminary Design Review (PDR) at the end of 2026, with the goal of having an initial flight software implementation by the end of spring 2027.
